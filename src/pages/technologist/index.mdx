### Smart Home + IoT Automation
I’m an **IoT enthusiast** who builds connected systems that reduce waste, save money, and make homes more personal and responsive.

- Automated lighting for comfort and energy efficiency

Smart homes aren’t just convenience—they’re **awareness**, **control**, and **efficiency**.
