# Raheem Williams — Chief Compliance Officer  
### The Standard Behind the Mission

> Raheem Williams understands that when people depend on the work you do, **doing it right isn't optional—and doing it right later isn't good enough.**

That mindset has been shaped through years of working within Detroit's water system, where the work of treating and managing water directly impacts communities, families, businesses, and entire cities. His career has included multiple levels of water plant operations, giving him firsthand experience in an environment where **standards, procedures, certifications, performance, documentation, and accountability matter every single day**.

Raheem understands that compliance isn't paperwork for the sake of paperwork. **Standards exist because people are depending on you to meet them.**

His background also extends beyond water operations. With an education in Mechanical Engineering and earlier experience in engineering, technology, telecommunications, and education, Raheem brings a technical and analytical mindset to understanding how systems are supposed to operate.

As our **Chief Compliance Officer**, Raheem brings that discipline to the organization. He examines our policies, processes, performance, documentation, and responsibilities to make sure we're not simply saying we're operating correctly—we can **demonstrate that we're operating correctly**.

He understands how to work within environments shaped by government requirements, organizational policies, certifications, reviews, and industry standards. But his value goes beyond checking boxes. Raheem looks at whether the standards we're creating internally are strong enough to hold ourselves accountable even when no one else is watching.

His philosophy can be summed up simply:

**Don't just do it right. Do it right now.**

Because compliance isn't something you fix when someone comes looking. **It's a standard you build into how you operate from the beginning.**
