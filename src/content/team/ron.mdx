# Ronald Jackson - Chief Information Officer
### The Architect of the Information

> Ron the Architect and he's is all about make sure thing make sense, and you understand what he's delivering. 

Ron Jackson brings an engineer’s mindset to the flow of information. As an Electrical Engineer with a master’s degree and 25 years of experience at Ford Motor Company, Ron has built his career around being thorough, methodical, and prepared for what could go wrong—not just what should go right.

His experience spans circuit analysis, supplier and vendor relationships, quality assurance, troubleshooting, and long-term reliability. Whether evaluating a circuit or working with suppliers, Ron understands that every component matters. A system is only as dependable as the thought, testing, and quality built into it.

As our Chief Information Officer, Ron applies that same discipline to information. He looks at how information enters the organization, where it travels, who it reaches, what depends on it, and where potential points of failure exist. His approach includes something engineers understand well: worst-case analysis. Don't just design for when everything works—understand what happens when it doesn't.

Ron brings a critical question to the team: Will what we're building still work when it's tested?

His responsibility is to help ensure our information systems are reliable, secure, efficient, and built to last. Because whether it's a circuit, a supply chain, or the flow of information, Ron understands that quality isn't measured by how well something works today—it's measured by whether you can depend on it tomorrow.
