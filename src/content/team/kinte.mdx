# Kinte Seay — Chief Financial Officer
### The Strategist Behind the Numbers

> Kinte is a numbers, based on his planning and power background has a great understanding of finances to find ways to retire 15 year early and find value in savings.

Kinte Seay is the strategist behind the numbers. An engineer by education and a planner by nature, Kinte has built his career understanding complex systems, anticipating what could happen next, and making calculated decisions to produce the best possible outcome.

With a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from Kettering University and NERC certification, Kinte's professional journey has taken him from telematics engineering at Motorola to power-system planning and operations at DTE Energy and Georgia Power. His work in the energy industry has required him to think beyond the immediate problem—to understand capacity, reliability, risk, resources, and what happens to an entire system when one variable changes.

He brings that same mindset to money.

Kinte doesn't simply look at what something costs today. He wants to know what that decision will be worth tomorrow. He studies the variables, creates scenarios, compares outcomes, and looks for the strategy that produces the greatest long-term value.

That approach has shaped his own financial journey. Through disciplined budgeting, investing, tax planning, and retirement modeling, Kinte developed a strategy designed to position himself to retire approximately 15 years earlier than the traditional retirement timeline. Whether evaluating Roth IRA investments, maximizing 401(k) contributions, reducing taxable retirement income, or deciding where the next dollar should go, his philosophy is to run the numbers before making the move.

He has taken that knowledge beyond his own finances by helping small-business owners better understand their bookkeeping and financial position. For Kinte, knowing how much money came in and went out isn't enough. Business owners need to understand what their numbers are telling them and how to use those numbers to make their next decision.

As our Chief Financial Officer, Kinte brings the discipline of an engineer, the foresight of a planner, and the mindset of an investor to our financial strategy.

His question isn't simply, “Can we afford it?”

It's “If we do this today, where does it put us tomorrow?”

Because Kinte understands that the goal isn't just to make money—it's to strategically position every dollar to create more opportunity, more stability, and more value.
