# Kinte Seay Senior Project Manager/EE

> Kinte is a numbers, based on his planning and power background has a great understanding of finances to find ways to retire 15 year early and find value in savings.

Kinte earned a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from Kettering University in 
1999 and holds NERC Reliability Coordinator Certification.

Kinte begain his career as a Telematics Applications Engineer for Motorola. Kinte next moved 
to DTE Energy in Detroit, MI, where he worked as an engineer in Sub-transmission Planning 
and as a System Supervisor operating the distribution system for DTE Energy. In 2013, Kinte 
joined the Georgia Power Transmission Control Center as a Power System Coordinator and in 
2016, he moved to a System Operator position, overseeing reliable transmission operations for 
Georgia Power and the Georgia ITS.
