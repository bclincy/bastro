# Shunte McMillian - Chief Data Officer
### The Life of the Insight

>  Shunte is the life of the insight. As a DJ, he has honed the ability to read the room—knowing what people need, when they need it, and understanding that what they need isn’t always what they want in the moment.

As our Chief Data Officer, Shunte brings that same instinct to data. He understands that collecting information is only the beginning. The real value comes from knowing who the data belongs to, who should have access to it, why it should be used, when it should be activated, and how it can be leveraged responsibly.

Just as a great DJ knows the right record can change the energy of an entire room, Shunte understands that the right insight, delivered at the right time, can change a decision, shape a strategy, and move a community forward.

His role is to make sure our data remains protected, purposeful, and powerful—turning information into insight without losing sight of the people behind it.

> little more about Shunte

Shunte McMillian is a Salesforce Architect, Product Owner, and technical project leader with extensive experience delivering enterprise Salesforce and CRM solutions. A Michigan State University graduate with an MBA, Shunte combines business strategy with technical execution to design scalable, high-impact platforms that improve revenue performance and operational efficiency.

He holds numerous industry certifications including Salesforce Certified Application Architect, Platform Developer I, Platform Data Architect, Platform Sharing & Visibility Architect, Revenue Cloud Consultant, Service Cloud Consultant, and Sales Cloud Consultant, along with PMP, Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO), and SAFe Product Owner/Product 
Manager certifications.

Shunte is known for leading cross-functional teams in Agile environments, architecting complex integrations, and guiding organizations through digital transformation initiatives across the Salesforce ecosystem.
