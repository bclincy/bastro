Shunte McMillian is a Salesforce Architect, Product Owner, and technical project leader with extensive experience 
delivering enterprise Salesforce and CRM solutions. A Michigan State University graduate with an MBA, Shunte combines 
business strategy with technical execution to design scalable, high-impact platforms that improve revenue performance 
and operational efficiency.

He holds numerous industry certifications including Salesforce Certified Application Architect, Platform Developer I, 
Platform Data Architect, Platform Sharing & Visibility Architect, Revenue Cloud Consultant, Service Cloud Consultant, 
and Sales Cloud Consultant, along with PMP, Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO), and SAFe Product Owner/Product 
Manager certifications.

Shunte is known for leading cross-functional teams in Agile environments, architecting complex integrations, and guiding 
organizations through digital transformation initiatives across the Salesforce ecosystem.

Beyond technology, Shunte is also a professional DJ who has rocked parties coast to coast, bringing energy to weddings, 
celebrity events, and major celebrations.
